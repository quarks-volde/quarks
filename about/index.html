About me

I am a climate scientist and Project Scientist-I at the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune. My work spans coupled Earth-system modelling, tropical climate dynamics, monsoon variability, decadal prediction, and data-driven climate research.

My research brings together physical model development and climate diagnostics, from CICE6 and UFS to MJO–tropical cyclone interactions and Pacific–monsoon teleconnections. I am also a PhD research scholar at CORAL, IIT Kharagpur, working on the influence of the Madden–Julian Oscillation on tropical cyclonic activity over the Indian and Pacific Oceans.
