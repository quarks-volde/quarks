03 / Decadal variability & teleconnections

DCPP-C experiments & teleconnections

DCPP-C is Component C of the Decadal Climate Prediction Project (DCPP), coordinated within the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP). It focuses on predictability, physical mechanisms and case studies of climate variations and shifts on annual-to-decadal timescales.

I carried out CMIP7 DCPP-C experiments with the IITM Decadal Climate Prediction System (IITM-DCPS) to examine how North Pacific and Southern Ocean surface forcing influences decadal climate variability and atmospheric teleconnections.

Each integration spans 20 years: 10 years with imposed surface forcing, followed by 10 years of free evolution. Positive and negative forcing experiments are compared to study the climate response and its persistence.

North Pacific
Aleutian Low–based momentum and buoyancy forcing, using a 20-member ensemble.
Southern Ocean · combined forcing
Momentum and buoyancy flux perturbations to study the coupled response.
Southern Ocean · wind stress
Zonal wind-stress forcing to isolate the response to momentum forcing.
Southern Ocean · freshwater
Precipitation minus evaporation (P–E) forcing, using a 10-member ensemble.

I analyse sea-surface temperature, surface winds, atmospheric circulation and PDO-like variability. This work also supports my study of Aleutian Low forcing and Indian summer monsoon teleconnections.

04 / Tropical climate & coupled air–sea processes

MJO–tropical cyclone interactions

Research on the basin-dependent influence of the Madden–Julian Oscillation on tropical-cyclone genesis and activity across the Indo-Pacific, using observations, climate diagnostics, and COAWST/ROMS–WRF modelling.

My PhD research examines MJO influence on tropical cyclonic activity over the Indian and Pacific Oceans, alongside monsoon–cyclogenesis interactions.

View the MJO phase reference map

Earlier ocean-modelling work at IIT Kharagpur investigated shortwave penetration and upper-ocean response in the Bay of Bengal using satellite optical datasets and ROMS.

My co-authored studies examine cyclone-driven cold wakes in the Arabian Sea and the simultaneous occurrence of cyclones Kyarr and Maha. A 2023 EGU abstract extends the MJO analysis to the western North Pacific.
