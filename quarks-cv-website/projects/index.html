01 / Earth-system modelling

UFS / GFS v16 – IITM-ESM

Work on UFS/S2SWAL and FV3/GFS v16 integration planning, component interfaces, and coupled-model validation for IITM-ESM.

MODEL DEVELOPMENT HIGHLIGHT

Compiled the coupled UFS framework and established the software and dependency environment for coupled-model testing on IITM ARKA HPC.

This work draws on coupling and HPC tools including ESMF, NUOPC, CMEPS/FMS, MPI/OpenMP, Linux HPC, and PBS.

02 / Sea-ice model development

CICE6 implementation & testing

CICE6 configuration, climatological integrations, restart testing, and preparation for coupled-model use on IITM HPC.

COMPLETED INTEGRATIONS

Completed long climatological integrations at 100 km and 25 km resolution, with restart, reproducibility, and diagnostic checks.

The model workflow includes compilation, dependency management, namelist configuration, and output validation.

03 / Decadal prediction & teleconnections

North Pacific forcing & the Indian monsoon

DCPP-C research on North Pacific forcing, PDO-like variability, and teleconnection pathways to Indian summer monsoon rainfall (ISMR).

Current work examines the impact of Aleutian Low-induced momentum and buoyancy forcing on the Indian summer monsoon and associated teleconnection pathways.

04 / Tropical climate & coupled air–sea processes

MJO–tropical cyclone interactions

Research on the basin-dependent influence of the Madden–Julian Oscillation on tropical-cyclone genesis and activity across the Indo-Pacific, using observations, climate diagnostics, and COAWST/ROMS–WRF modelling.

My PhD research examines MJO influence on tropical cyclonic activity over the Indian and Pacific Oceans, alongside monsoon–cyclogenesis interactions.

Earlier ocean-modelling work at IIT Kharagpur investigated shortwave penetration and upper-ocean response in the Bay of Bengal using satellite optical datasets and ROMS.

05 / Indo-Pacific climate variability

Indo-Pacific warm-pool change

Research on warm-pool expansion and associated changes in MJO variability and tropical-cyclone response. This theme connects the ocean background state with large-scale tropical variability and regional cyclone activity.

06 / Climate AI & predictability

Explainable learning & probabilistic prediction

Explainable LSTM approaches to monsoon–cyclogenesis links and Bayesian CNN methods for probabilistic ENSO prediction.

Technical expertise includes LSTM, CNN/BCNN, SHAP, ensemble analysis, regression, filtering, composites, and significance tests.
